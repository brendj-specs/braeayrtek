Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Braeayrtek ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how our Windows desktop software handles your information.

1. Data Collection & Offline Operation

Our software operates entirely offline on your Windows device. We do not collect, transmit, track, or store your personal files, project data, or usage analytics on external servers.

2. Internet Connections

Once installed, the application requires no internet connection to run. During initial installation, the installer may connect directly to Microsoft's official servers solely to download and install the required Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable runtime component.

3. Third-Party Services

App distribution, licensing, and payments are handled directly by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's own privacy terms. We do not sell, rent, or share any user information with third parties.

4. Changes to This Policy

If we update our software to include online features in the future, we will update this page before those changes take effect.
